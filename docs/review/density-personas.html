New admin

Day 1. Knows RPM and git. Has not read the design.

Top 5

On Run detail, put the button in the yellow banner and demote Retry failed. On package-slurm, turn the banner into a commit → pin → build step list.

  4. Stop pilling normal states. current, clean, ✓ same version, local/served: same and all-green checks become muted text or a single "all ok" line. Drop zero-count pills.
  5. On Publish review, move the blocking advisory and its Accept for this publish checkbox right under the target cards. Collapse Checks, Signing details, Trust and What happens to one-line summaries.

Small screen

13-inch laptop, 1280x720, 110 % zoom, mild low vision.

Persona

I run builds and publishes on a 13-inch laptop at 1280x720, with browser zoom at 110%. That gives an effective viewport of about 1164x655 CSS px, which is still above the 1100px breakpoint, so every two-column layout stays two columns and gets narrower. My vision is mildly low: grey text at 11-12px and 11.5px pills cost me effort. I scan a page in a few seconds and look for the red thing and the button. I use the UI several times a week, mostly Overview, then Run detail or Publish.

Facts checked in CSS that the whole review relies on: - --c-text-3 #8a7f74 has a contrast of 3.91:1 on white, 3.69 on --c-bg, 3.39 on --c-surface-2 and 3.04 on --c-surface-3. It fails AA 4.5 for small text in every one of those places. It is used for .table th (12px on surface-2), .nav-label and .nav-item .count (11px), .card-head .right (12px), .meta-strip dt and .lbl (11px uppercase), .tile .k (11.5px uppercase), and many .note, .hint and .why texts (11.5-12px). - --s-skip #a79c91 is 2.69:1 on white. .cnts .z uses opacity .35 and .legend .pill.zero uses .55, which makes those items nearly invisible to me. - .pill is 20px high with 500 11.5px. .tag is 11.5px mono. .btn-sm is 26px high with 12px text. .nd .pill and .glegend .pill are 18px/11px, and .deplist .pill is 10.5px. - Body text is 14px. Most secondary text is 12-12.5px, and code, .mono is .92em, so mono inside 13px text comes out at about 12px. - Neither .dlg nor .dlg.rb has a max-height or overflow. Dialogs taller than the viewport get clipped at the top and bottom.
